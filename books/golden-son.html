Golden Son

by Pierce Brown

If you thought Red Rising couldn't get any more dramatic, think again! Faster, bigger, and much spacier. More plot twists! More action! Raaaaahhhh!

Easton told me this was the book Brown really wanted to write, and Red Rising was a publisher-pleaser to get his foot in the door. I can see it. Where Book 1 was quite Hunger Games-like, Golden Son is really something else.

There's definitely plot twists somewhere, but I won't try to look for them. And every character, including Darrow, seems blatantly like they serve the plot too. But once again the boy doesn't miss when it comes to plot. "Now it seems as if the [GoT] comparisons was coming true".

Starting to wish he'd write a longer sentence once in a while. But still, looking forward to the next one.
