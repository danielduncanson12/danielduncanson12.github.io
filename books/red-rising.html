Red Rising

by Pierce Brown

Don't let Netflix get their hands on this!!

It's been a very very long time since I read a book as addictive as this. Even on 5-minute bus rides I'd pull it out to see what would happen next. Think Hunger Games but faster and bigger and spacier.

It's definitely lacking in descriptions of environments and depths of characters. But man, this Brown fella can really write a plot. The world-building is phenomenal. Storylines are layered and intertwine. There's secrets and betrayal and twists everywhere.

It's like the perfect book for Gen Z. Chapters are rarely more than 10 pages long and there's a cliff-hanger or overdramatic exclamation at the end of every single one. The average sentence is about one line, and there's never three chapters in a row without some action. If your favourite book comes from 19th century Russia, maybe not for you.

I keep wondering if it would work better as a movie or tv series. So far my conclusion is this: as a movie, it'll be a huge big-budget hit but you'll have to cut out a lot, ala Project Hail Mary. As a TV series, it could be the next GoT, but it'd need a lot of money and talented people.
